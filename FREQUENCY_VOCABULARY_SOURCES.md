# Frequency vocabulary sources and attribution

The frequency drill retains its original curated entries and adds **1,000 new everyday German words**.
New additions are drawn from the app's existing German–Czech noun catalogue, verb lessons and curated
spoken-word additions. Words are selected by their rank in the German OpenSubtitles 2018 frequency list.

- Source project: [hermitdave/FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords)
- Corpus: OpenSubtitles 2018 German
- Source file: `content/2018/de/de_50k.txt`

## Approximate levels

The new entries are assigned to frequency-informed study bands:

| App level | Corpus frequency rank |
| --- | ---: |
| A1 | 1–1,000 |
| A2 | 1,001–4,000 |
| B1 | 4,001–10,000 |
| B2 | 10,001 and above |

These bands are **not official Goethe-Institut CEFR classifications**. The corpus consists of subtitles,
so it is a practical proxy for conversational language exposure, not a perfect measurement of all
spoken German. Existing manually curated entries keep their original A1–B2 assignment.

The German–Czech meanings and the example sentences in the added entries come from existing
app-maintained learning content or curated additions. The source frequency list is used for word
selection and level estimation, not for Czech translation.
