# DeutschAI

**Learn German your way. Learn at your own pace.**

DeutschAI is an offline-first Android application for learning German, designed especially for Czech-speaking learners.

## Our vision

A personal German tutor that helps you learn naturally, understand your mistakes, and build confidence in real conversations.

## Planned features

- **CEFR learning paths:** A1, A2, B1, B2 and C1.
- **Personal tutor:** Beatrice, a friendly virtual German teacher.
- **Grammar explained in Czech:** Clear explanations with practical examples.
- **Vocabulary training:** Learn, review and retain new words.
- **Conversation practice:** Realistic German dialogues and corrections.
- **Spaced repetition:** Review vocabulary at the right time.
- **Offline-first:** Core lessons and progress tracking available without an internet connection.
- **Privacy-focused:** Learning progress stored locally on the device.
- **Business German:** Practical language for professional situations.

## Technology

- Kotlin
- Android Jetpack Compose
- Room database
- Local AI integration planned for a future version

## Development status

Early planning and prototype stage. Features will be implemented and tested incrementally.

## Principles

- No advertisements.
- No forced gamification.
- No mandatory online AI subscription.
- Transparent distinction between scripted exercises and AI-generated conversations.
- Focus on practical learning and long-term progress.
